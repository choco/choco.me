The doodle set, on a 32 grid with 2.5px round strokes: `sprinkle.svg` (filled), `swirl.svg`, `heart.svg`, `cocoa-bean.svg`, `squiggle.svg`, `daisy.svg`, `dots.svg` (filled), `pizza.svg` (a slice, its pepperoni filled), `chocolate.svg` (a bitten bar) and `arrow-curl.svg`, the arrow every `Note` points with. The files are single-ink in `cocoa` (`#3d2520`); on a page, draw them inline in `currentColor` and colour them as the README's Colour section says, since an `<img>` cannot take a colour.
